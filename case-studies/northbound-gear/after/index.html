Frequently
Asked Questions

How stretchy are the Adventure Pants waist?

The waistband has a stretch of about 2–3 inches for extra comfort and mobility.

Which colors come with an orange zipper?

Black and Dark Gray come with an orange zipper. Army Green and APX Camo do not.

What temperature range are they best for?

They’re most comfortable in temperatures from -15°C to 20°C (5°F to 68°F).

Are they regular fit or slim fit?

The waist runs true to size and the leg tapers to a slim calf. Sizing up won't add calf room; it just makes the waist loose.
