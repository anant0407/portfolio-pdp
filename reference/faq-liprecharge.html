Frequently Asked Questions

WHAT IS THE FINISH OF LIP RECHARGE?

Lip Recharge provides a soft shine finish. It's not as glossy as a traditional lip gloss, but leaves lips with a subtle amount of shine that looks healthy and hydrated.

IS LIP RECHARGE SCENTED OR DOES IT HAVE A FLAVOR?

Lip Recharge features a subtle vanilla mint scent.

HOW DO I KNOW WHICH SHADE IS RIGHT FOR ME?

Lip Recharge comes in 9 universal, casually sheer shades that anyone can wear. Even shades that look brighter, like Lilac, will sheer out once applied and leave a wash of color that never looks overdone. You can't go wrong with these shades.

WHAT MAKES LIP RECHARGE DIFFERENT FROM TRADITIONAL LIP GLOSS?

Lip Recharge is different from traditional lip glosses in that it features a more skincare-forward formula than a cosmetic lip product does. It's powered by high-performance ingredients like moisture-boosting peptides, vitamin E, and shea butter. While it does add a flattering hint of color to your looks, it's also working hard to hydrate, smooth, and soften lips as you wear it.
