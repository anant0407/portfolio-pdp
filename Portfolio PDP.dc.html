The product

One designer who has already made the mistake you are about to make.

Twelve years across fintech, logistics and health tools — most of it as the only designer in the room. I write the research plan, draw the screens, argue about the edge cases, and hand engineering something they can build from on Monday.

Most teams do not need a design department. They need one person who can hold the whole problem — the messy business constraint, the thing the data actually says, the interface that has to ship in six weeks — and keep it coherent while everyone else does their job.

That is the entire offer. No pods, no discovery theatre, no deck that arrives three weeks late. You get the work, in your tools, at your cadence.
